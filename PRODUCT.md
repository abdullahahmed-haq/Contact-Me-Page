## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal contact hub for Abdullah Ahmed. Visitors explore configured destinations, compose WhatsApp messages, download a contact card, and scan a configured public URL.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The approved public profile, badges, featured website, six initial contact cards, QR and add-to-contacts layout are preserved. New cards are explicitly configured and enabled; no invented destinations or usage claims. Local development uses persistent PGlite; production requires managed PostgreSQL and persistent object storage.
